# reverb_time_low
The time in seconds the low frequencies of the reverb heard by this sound take to decay by 60 decibels.

`const float reverb_time_low;`

## Remarks:
This is estimated from the reflections of the sound's group, and is 0 until they have been simulated. A small furnished room has a short reverb time, while a large hard room has a long one.
