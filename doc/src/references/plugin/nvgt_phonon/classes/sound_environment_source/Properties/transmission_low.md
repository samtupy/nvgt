# transmission_low
How much of the low frequencies (below about 800 hertz) of the sound passes through the obstacles between it and the listener, from 0 to 1.

`const float transmission_low;`

## Remarks:
The transmission values describe what remains of an occluded sound, and are 1 when nothing is in the way. A brick wall, for example, lets through only about 1.5 percent of the sound at every frequency, while thin materials let through more of the low end than the high end.
